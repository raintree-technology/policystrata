# PolicyStrata OSS Package TODO

PolicyStrata is the open-source package and local evidence/runtime engine.
Clearance / agent-assurance is the hosted app on policystrata.com.

Root-level TODO stays here for OSS package work. See `docs/oss-todo-policy.md`.
Hosted app work belongs in the private `raintree-technology/agent-assurance` repository.
Stable OSS contract issue/milestone labels are in `docs/contract-milestones.md`.
Paper and evaluation-evidence follow-ups are in `PAPER_TODO.md`, not here.

Use the split like Pydantic and Logfire:

- PolicyStrata OSS: local runner, SDKs, gateway, schemas, deterministic tests, evidence artifacts.
- Clearance app: hosted orgs, auth, RBAC, review UI, waivers, approvals, audit logs, procurement, billing, trust center.
