\section{Conclusion}

\policystrata{} treats a data agent as a policy pipeline whose representations are related but not equal. Its transition contracts separate an upstream lowering error, downstream containment, and final release instead of collapsing them into one pass/fail answer. The artifact covers its declared 22-operator model, exposes gaps missed by specification-derived tests, and emits intervention-checked witnesses for the first failed transition. Spec-blind misses, null brownfield discovery, and silent adapter mutations show where the evidence stops. The result is a reproducible method for policy-drift regression testing. It does not establish production recall and does not replace enforcement.
