# DAI 2026 Readiness

Official page: https://www.adai.ai/dai/2026/research-track.html

Abstract deadline: 2026-07-27. Paper deadline: 2026-08-03.

## Current Status

Hold unless we commit to a larger rewrite.

DAI wants original, rigorous work in distributed AI, autonomous agents, multi-agent systems, and
agentic AI. PolicyStrata can fit, but only if rewritten as an agentic-AI systems/evaluation paper
with broader agent framing and stronger double-blind preparation.

## Minimum Upgrade Before Submission

- Anonymous 8-page ACM paper.
- At least one more real integration or broader agent benchmark evidence.
- Agent engineering and infrastructure framing.
- Ethical/legal/societal considerations.
- Explicit generative-AI-use disclosure.

## Decision Rule

Do not submit DAI while SECUTE or another substantially similar archival paper is under review.
