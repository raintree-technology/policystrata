# AgenticDev 2026 Submission Plan

Official page: https://conf.researchr.org/home/ase-2026/agenticdev-2026

Deadline: 2026-07-15.

## Recommended Use

Backup or alternate to SECUTE, not a simultaneous duplicate archival submission.

## Product

5-page anonymous ACM demo/tool paper.

## Framing

PolicyStrata tests policy-bearing workflows in LLM agents that use private structured-data tools.
Lead with agentic software workflows: model-visible tools, semantic plans, trace export, validators,
compiler/lowering, CI evidence, and human-readable witnesses.

## Required Differentiation From SECUTE

- Less adversary language.
- More agent workflow and toolchain language.
- Emphasize trace adapters, agent integration points, and CI adoption.
- Use one real trace-adapter walkthrough: Next.js/Drizzle, Prisma, or SQLAlchemy.

## Hold Criteria

Do not submit if SECUTE is active unless the paper is substantially rewritten and the artifact claims
are different enough to avoid overlap.
