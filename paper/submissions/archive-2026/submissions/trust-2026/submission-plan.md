# TRUST 2026 Submission Plan

Official page: https://conf.researchr.org/home/ase-2026/trust-2026

Deadline: 2026-07-23.

## Product

4-page anonymous short paper or 5-8 page anonymous experience report.

## Framing

PolicyStrata continuously assures trustworthy and responsible data-agent behavior by checking
governance, auditability, policy/TOS/privacy obligations, human-readable witnesses, and CI release
gates.

## Required Differentiation

- Lead with governance and accountability, not security exploits.
- Emphasize policy document coverage, release policies, and audit evidence.
- Include an explicit limitation that doctor mode classifies obligation signals but is not legal review.

## Conflict Rule

Use as a backup unless it becomes a distinct governance/experience paper. Do not duplicate SECUTE.
