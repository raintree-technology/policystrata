\begin{abstract}
\policystrata{} tests whether policy obligations remain intact as an LLM data agent moves from a model-visible capability to a released result. These obligations recur across capability manifests, validators, query compilers, database controls, and result-release logic. Each component may pass its own tests even when a mismatch between components exposes the wrong capability, produces unauthorized SQL, or releases a disallowed result.

\policystrata{} is a deterministic regression tester for this cross-layer policy drift. It checks each transition in execution order and emits a replayable witness for the first violated contract. Its v1 benchmark defines 22 mutation operators across three synthetic analytics domains. \policystrata{} detected all 1,720 represented fault cases and emitted no witness for 80 clean controls. This result measures coverage of the implemented fault model, not recall on unknown production failures. A conventional specification-derived suite missed 141 cases, a layered point-control stack missed 159, and a spec-blind suite agreed with \policystrata{} on 39 of 42 cases.

Separate studies test the method beyond its generated fixtures. A scan of 75 real-SQL traces from four independently maintained open-source systems produced one content-level false positive and exposed five adapter gaps, which were fixed. In PostgreSQL, 20 upstream row-level-security policies passed 13 containment checks. Weakening one predicate failed only the checks that covered it. A frozen MetricFlow revision reproduced 68 upstream-authored expected-SQL cases byte-for-byte. A maintainer-operated deployment study matched inspected source to the deployed revision and passed 33 read-only denial-boundary probes; three authenticated probes were unavailable. Private-source replay reproduced three historical fixes. Together, these studies establish a reproducible fault-model result and a concrete retargeting path. They do not establish field recall or independently operated deployment effectiveness.
\end{abstract}
